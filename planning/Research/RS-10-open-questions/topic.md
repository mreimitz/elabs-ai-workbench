# Product Open Questions

## Objective

Keep the unresolved product questions from the original planning phase in one place, so a decision is recorded rather than re-argued.

## Why now / what it feeds

The original planning documents raised questions that outlived them and were being answered inconsistently.

## Scope

**In:** The open questions recorded during the original MVP planning.

**Out:** Questions already resolved into locked decisions inside a roadmap item.

## Deliverable

A live list of open product questions.

## Success criteria

Each question is either answered with a pointer to where the answer lives, or visibly still open.
