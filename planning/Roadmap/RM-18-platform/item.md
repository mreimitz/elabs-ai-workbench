## Invariants

- Demo/seed data is clearly labeled, removable in one action, and never mixes with real data.
- The diagnostics bundle is proven secret-free by an automated test (same discipline as PAT
  redaction), not by review.
- Performance budgets are asserted numbers in tests, not aspirations in prose.
- 1.4's fixture DBs are committed binary artifacts — small, generated by script, documented.
